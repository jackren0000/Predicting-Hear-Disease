# Predicting Heart Disease

**Introduction**
The World Health Organization (WHO) estimates that 17.9 millio people die from cardiovascular diseases (CVDs) every year.
Thus identifying the risk factors early on is essential of preventing premature deaths.
We are aiming to use dataset to accurately predict the likelihood of a new patient having heart disease in the future.

**Dataset**
The dataset includes relevant information for each patients, such as their personal information and some medical data, including whether or not they have had heart disease before.
